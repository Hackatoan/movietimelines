Godzilla watch order — FAQ

What order should I watch Godzilla in?

In in-universe timeline order: Kong: Skull Island, Godzilla, Monarch: Legacy of Monsters, Godzilla: King of the Monsters, Godzilla vs. Kong, Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire. The full list above is in story order (monsterverse ordered by in-story setting; toho by release).

How many Godzilla movies and shows are there?

39 films and 1 series in the main timeline and optional extras combined (40 entries in total).

How long does it take to watch all of Godzilla?

About 74 hours back to back, counting every film's runtime and every episode at its average length.

Do I need to watch everything?

No. The core entries are the main story; Toho & classic (the Japanese Toho films (1954–2023) + the 1998 US film — separate continuities) are optional and can be toggled on in the tracker.
