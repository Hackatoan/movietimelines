Transformers watch order — FAQ

What order should I watch Transformers in?

In in-universe timeline order: Transformers One, Bumblebee, Transformers: Rise of the Beasts, Transformers, Transformers: Revenge of the Fallen, Transformers: Dark of the Moon, and so on. The full list above is in story order (order by in-story setting — live-action continuity is loose).

How many Transformers movies and shows are there?

9 films and 1 series in the main timeline and optional extras combined (10 entries in total).

How long does it take to watch all of Transformers?

About 56 hours back to back, counting every film's runtime and every episode at its average length.

Do I need to watch everything?

No. The core entries are the main story; G1 classics (the original 1984–86 Generation 1 cartoon & movie) are optional and can be toggled on in the tracker.
