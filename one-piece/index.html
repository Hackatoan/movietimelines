One Piece watch order — FAQ

What order should I watch One Piece in?

In in-universe timeline order: East Blue Saga, Alabasta Saga, Sky Island Saga, Water 7 Saga, Thriller Bark Saga, Summit War Saga, and so on. The full list above is in story order (grouped by saga · ~24 min/episode).

How many One Piece movies and shows are there?

15 films and 11 series in the main timeline and optional extras combined (26 entries in total).

How long does it take to watch all of One Piece?

About 493 hours back to back, counting every film's runtime and every episode at its average length.

Do I need to watch everything?

No. The core entries are the main story; Movies (the 15 theatrical films (mostly standalone side-stories)) are optional and can be toggled on in the tracker.
