Pokémon watch order — FAQ

What order should I watch Pokémon in?

In in-universe timeline order: Pokémon: The Original Series, Pokémon: Advanced Generation, Pokémon: Diamond and Pearl, Pokémon: Black & White, Pokémon the Series: XY, Pokémon the Series: Sun & Moon, and so on. The full list above is in story order (english-dub seasons, in order).

How many Pokémon movies and shows are there?

24 films and 13 series in the main timeline and optional extras combined (37 entries in total).

How long does it take to watch all of Pokémon?

About 541 hours back to back, counting every film's runtime and every episode at its average length.

Do I need to watch everything?

No. The core entries are the main story; Movies (the theatrical anime films (mostly standalone; later ones are a separate reboot timeline)); Spin-offs (Detective Pikachu, plus the web/side series (Origins, Generations, Concierge…)) are optional and can be toggled on in the tracker.
