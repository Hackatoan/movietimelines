Dragon Ball watch order — FAQ

What order should I watch Dragon Ball in?

In in-universe timeline order: Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball GT, Dragon Ball Super, Dragon Ball Daima. The full list above is in story order (release order · ~23 min/episode).

How many Dragon Ball movies and shows are there?

20 films and 6 series in the main timeline and optional extras combined (26 entries in total).

How long does it take to watch all of Dragon Ball?

About 347 hours back to back, counting every film's runtime and every episode at its average length.

Do I need to watch everything?

No. The core entries are the main story; Kai (remaster) (Dragon Ball Z Kai — a re-edited, faster-paced version of Z (watch instead of Z, not as well)); Movies (the theatrical films (mostly standalone; modern ones are canon-ish)) are optional and can be toggled on in the tracker.
